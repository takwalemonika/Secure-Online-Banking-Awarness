🔐 Essential Banking Safety Tips

Follow these simple rules whenever you use online banking.

🔑

Strong Password

Use a strong and unique password. Never reuse your banking password on other websites.

🔢

Protect OTP

Never share OTP, PIN, CVV or password with anyone, even if they claim to be a bank employee.

📲

UPI Safety

Do not enter your UPI PIN just to receive money. Always check the transaction details before paying.

🌐

Check Website

Access banking services through the official bank website or official mobile application.

📶

Avoid Unsafe Wi-Fi

Avoid sensitive banking transactions on unsecured public Wi-Fi networks.

🔔

Enable Alerts

Keep transaction SMS, email and app notifications enabled.

🚨 Common Online Banking Scams

Learn how scammers try to deceive users.

📞

Fake Bank Calls

Fraudsters may pretend to be bank employees and ask for OTP, PIN or CVV.

💬

Fake SMS / Messages

Messages may claim your account will be blocked and ask you to click a suspicious link.

🎁

Prize & Lottery Scams

Never send money or banking information to claim an unexpected prize.

📱

Remote Access Scams

Never install unknown remote-access applications because an unknown caller asks you to.

✅ Do's and ❌ Don'ts

✅ DO

✔ Use official banking applications.

✔ Keep your phone and apps updated.

✔ Use strong passwords.

❌ DON'T

🆘 What To Do If You Suspect Fraud?

Act quickly if you notice an unauthorized transaction.

1

Contact Your Bank

Immediately contact your bank using its official customer-care channel.

2

Secure Your Account

Follow your bank's instructions to block cards, change credentials or secure the account.

3

Report Cyber Fraud

Report suspected cyber financial fraud through the appropriate official channels.

4

Save Evidence

Keep transaction details, screenshots, messages and other relevant information.
